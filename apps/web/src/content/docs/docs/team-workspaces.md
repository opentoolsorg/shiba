---
title: Work with your team
description: Keep personal and team work separate, share context deliberately, and grant employees only the access they need.
---

A team workspace brings people, AI employee profiles, and shared work into one place. Membership, shared context, connected accounts, and permission to act are separate controls.

## Choose the right workspace

Use the workspace switcher before creating an employee, saving a skill, or starting team work. Personal records stay in your personal scope; team records belong to the selected workspace.

Check the workspace name when something seems missing. Switching workspaces does not move private conversations, skills, or account credentials into the team.

## Invite people deliberately

Use **Team** to manage the workspace and its members. The available invitation and management controls depend on your role.

Joining a workspace does not automatically grant the member every connected account, the operator's computer, or the operator's model credentials and billing. Execution and app access still need their own configured permissions.

## Share the context a task needs

Use the collaboration-room controls to share work with the intended audience. Check who can read the room before adding notes or reviewing a saved task there.

Opening a saved task for review in a room presents its brief before use. Confirm the destination and contents. Using it creates an editable copy, not a move or a synchronized task; later setup edits do not update that copy. Sharing a brief does not start execution or grant access to the private sources behind it.

An employee profile is a reusable role, not an automatic bridge between everyone's private conversations. Keep sensitive personal information out of shared instructions unless that audience should receive it.

## Connect team-owned tools

A workspace app account has its own owner/admin setup and consent. It does not reuse a member's private account just because they joined the team.

Grant access to the specific member, employee, and resources needed for the job. A connection, a grant, and an action approval each serve a different purpose. See [Connections and tools](/docs/connections/).

## Review work together

Keep the brief, result, and follow-up decisions in the right thread or room. An authorized person still needs to approve the specific workspace run or external action when required.

Start with one employee and one reviewable task before expanding the workflow. Multiple profiles do not establish an unattended team or automatic handoffs between employees.
