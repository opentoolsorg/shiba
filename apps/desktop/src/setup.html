Connect to Shiba

Shiba Desktop opens your dashboard and keeps the approval queue in the tray. Choose where your work runs. Signing in, connecting a provider, and approving runs all happen in your browser on the server you pick.

Deployment

Use the https:// address of your Shiba deployment. Local development may use http://localhost.

This window stores only the server address. Your account, provider credentials, and approvals stay in the dashboard.
